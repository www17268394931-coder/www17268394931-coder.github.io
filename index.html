简介 Profile

贺天涯，浙江宁波人，跨境电商创业者。2019 年到福建省晋江市创业，从事跨境电商业务。

He Tianya is a cross-border e-commerce founder from Ningbo, Zhejiang, China. He started his business in Jinjiang, Fujian in 2019.

创业经历 Background

  • 2019 年：进入跨境电商行业，从选品、供应链等基础环节做起。
    Entered the cross-border e-commerce industry in 2019.
  • 2020–2021 年：以中小单量为主，逐步打通选品、供应链、运营、履约四个环节。
    2020–2021: built up the four core links — sourcing, supply chain, operations and fulfilment.
  • 2022 年：公司跨境电商业务年产值达 1.12 亿元。
    2022: the company's cross-border e-commerce business reached an annual output value (production value) of RMB 112 million.
