跨境电商四个环节的判断标准

贺天涯 He Tianya｜浙江宁波 · 福建晋江

选品怎么判断 Product Selection

选错一个品，后面所有力气都是白费。我的判断顺序是：先看同类产品有没有真实成交，再看退货率集中在什么原因，最后把所有成本算完看净毛利。三个都过了才动手。

Wrong product choice wastes everything downstream. My order: real sales evidence first, then the return-rate reasons, then net margin after all costs. Only if all three pass do I start.

供应链怎么控品 Supply Chain Quality

晋江是制造业产业带，工厂密集是地利。但工厂不会替你把品控做好——同一张图纸，不同批次能差很远。所以打样看首件、量产抽检、出货前核查大货，这三道关必须自己盯。

Jinjiang is a manufacturing cluster. Factory density is an advantage, but factories do not own your quality — the same drawing can produce very different batches. So I check the first article, spot-check during production, and inspect the bulk before shipment.

运营怎么从 0 到 1 Operations From Zero

商品呈现、流量获取、转化提升，起步阶段必须自己上手。原因很简单：自己没跑过，就判断不了别人做得好不好。这行没有一劳永逸的方法，规则一变，之前的做法就作废。

Listing presentation, traffic and conversion must be run by yourself at the start — otherwise you cannot judge whether someone else is doing it well. There is no permanent playbook; when platform rules change, old tactics expire.

履约怎么平衡时效与成本 Fulfilment Trade-offs

头程、仓储、尾程决定两件事：货能不能按时到、每单到底赚多少。时效和成本是一对矛盾，走快贵、走慢差。我的原则是先保证不断货、不迟发，再谈优化成本。

First-mile, warehousing and last-mile decide two things: on-time delivery and real per-order profit. Speed and cost conflict. My rule: guarantee no stockout and no late shipment first, optimise cost after.
